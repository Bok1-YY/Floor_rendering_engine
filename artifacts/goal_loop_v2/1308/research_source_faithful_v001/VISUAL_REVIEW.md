# Independent visual review — Layer 1 wall geometry

Disposition: **candidate accept for the isolated wall-only research layer**.

The top, northeast, and northwest renders were independently inspected against the canonical floor-plan image. The final views use neutral gray Workbench rendering with shadows disabled; the earlier large triangular shadow artifacts are absent. Framing contains the complete model, the identity source-XY orientation is not mirrored or rotated, and both axonometric views expose wall height and internal partitions.

The images intentionally contain no opening cuts, door leaves, windows, furniture, room labels, adjacency arrows, or IFC claims. Fully closed walls at opening locations are therefore a Layer-1 scope choice, not a claim that the source contains no doors.

The densest core area and the four source wall atoms shorter than 0.05 m are difficult to distinguish by eye, but the structural validation separately proves all 35 atom IDs are present with no duplicate or missing object. Future QA-only views may add atom/junction labels; this layer must not change wall geometry merely to improve visual readability.

Remaining limits: the 2.8 m height is an unverified research default, functional connectivity is not modeled, and formal build authorization remains false.
